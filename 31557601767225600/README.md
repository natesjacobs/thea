**+1.0 Gyr from now.** One billion years.

A round number rather than a physical threshold, which makes it a useful vantage point — it sits in the middle of the collapse rather than at any single edge of it.

What has already happened by here depends on which models you believe. The oxygen crash is close: Ozaki & Reinhard put it at 1.08 ± 0.14 Gyr, so it is either just ahead or just behind. C3 photosynthesis has failed. C4 plants may still hold on. The 1-D climate models have the oceans going now; the 3-D cloud models give Earth another half-billion years.

The Sun is about 9% brighter than today.

If the oxygen is already gone, there is no ozone layer and the sky passes unfiltered UV. If it is not, this is the last moment anyone could stand outside and breathe.
