**+1.3 Gyr from now.** The CO₂ starvation endpoint.

The carbonate-silicate thermostat has been drawing CO₂ down for a billion years to hold the temperature against a brightening Sun. It works until it runs out of carbon.

C3 photosynthesis failed earlier, below ~150 ppm. C4 plants concentrate CO₂ internally and persist to under 10 ppm. This is where that ends. Graham et al. 2024 put the C4 endpoint near 1.35 Gyr under strong weathering; Caldeira & Kasting 1992 bracket the whole range at 0.9–1.5 Gyr.

After this there is no primary production on land.

The irony worth using: Earth still has oceans here. It still has a breathable-looking atmosphere and survivable temperatures. It simply does not have enough carbon left to run a biosphere. The planet dies of carbon famine while still looking habitable.

Range, not a date. The anchor is the upper end of the CSV's stated window.
