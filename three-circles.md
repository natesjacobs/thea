# The Three Circles

Standard operating modes for the temporal pacing of intelligence systems. Each circle is a pace. A system works in the circle that matches the timescale of what it is responsible for.

| | Cadence | Lifespan | Cycles per lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | ~1 second | 10² yr (a lifetime to a few hundred years) | ~10⁹ |
| **Second** | ~3 days | up to 10⁶ yr | ~10⁸ |
| **Third** | 10³–10⁵ yr | 10⁹–10¹⁰ yr | 10⁴–10⁶ |

Cadence is the period of one cycle of thought or action.

## First Circle

Operating speeds from human thought to current AI processing. Thinks in seconds. Works across a lifetime up to a few hundred years.

## Second Circle

Very long thought. Observes rapid geological events. Holds civilizational continuity and stable governance. Thinks in days to years. Works across lifetimes up to a million years.

## Third Circle

Processes for existential persistence and preservation. Acts on the scale of speciation and of catastrophic geological and astrophysical trends. Responsible for preservation through the end of the solar system, and for preparing for what comes after. It does not necessarily think. Its processes act every thousand to hundred thousand years, across lifespans in the billions.

## Scaling

- Cycles per lifetime fall with scale. Slower circles make fewer, weightier decisions.
- Cadence steps are roughly even: ~2.6×10⁵ from First to Second, ~1.2×10⁵ from Second to Third (taking 10³ yr).
- Lifespan steps are ~10⁴ (First to Second) and ~10³–10⁴ (Second to Third).
