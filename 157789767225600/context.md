**+5 Myr from now.** The East African Rift, on the day the sentinel wakes AI-1.

No humans are present in the rift. Whether any exist elsewhere is not settled here. See `../characters/AI-1.md`.

## Known

- The rift runs about 3,500 km from Afar to Mozambique. Africa is splitting into the Nubian plate in the west and the Somali plate in the east, with the Victoria and Rovuma microplates caught between.
- Opening is fastest in the north: about 6 mm/yr in Afar and the Main Ethiopian Rift, falling to 1–2 mm/yr in Mozambique. Over 5 Myr that is ~30 km of new width in the north and under 10 km in the south.
- Afar is a triple junction. The Red Sea and Gulf of Aden ridges, spreading at roughly 15–20 mm/yr, meet the Ethiopian rift there. Crust under the Danakil Depression is already thinned to about 15 km, close to oceanic.
- The Danakil floor lies about 125 m below sea level. Lake Assal is at −155 m. Low volcanic sills keep the sea out. The Red Sea has broken in before, most recently about 130,000 years ago, and evaporated each time. The salt left behind is over a kilometre thick.
- Rifting moves in jumps. In 2005 a 60 km dike opened the Dabbahu segment by up to 8 m in weeks (Wright et al. 2006).
- Rifts often fail. The Anza rift in Kenya and the Benue Trough stretched, stopped, and filled with sediment. The Red Sea took about 25 Myr to go from first rifting to seafloor spreading.
- The floor of the Ethiopian rift is high, 1,600 m at its lakes. Lake Turkana, to the south, is low, at 360 m, over crust already thinned to about 20 km by the older Anza rift.
- Lake Victoria is shallow and young. It dried out completely about 17,000 years ago. Its ~500 cichlid species evolved since it refilled.
- So "ocean or failed rift" is open in the science. Extrapolation gives a sea in Afar within 5 Myr and little else decided.

### What happened

Times are from the start of the wait.

- **+9 kyr. First water.** It came from the east and not the north. A rifting episode split the lava sill between the Gulf of Tadjoura and Lake Assal. Seas stood about 20 m above today's. The gulf poured in for most of a decade. The salt pan dissolved.
- **+41 kyr. The false ocean.** The sill at Zula failed and the Red Sea flooded the whole Danakil, 10,000 km², in a few years. The sentinel did not wake. Water is not an ocean. The crust underneath was still stretched continent.
- **+63 kyr.** The Alid volcano rebuilt the sill. The cut-off sea, 120 m deep, evaporated in a century and left two metres of new salt.
- **To +1.1 Myr.** The Danakil filled and dried eleven times.
- **+1.1 Myr. The gulf stays.** The sills sank below the sea for good as the crust thinned. A permanent arm of the Red Sea: the Afar Gulf.
- **+1.4 Myr. First true ocean floor**, along the Erta Ale axis. Basalt records the direction of Earth's magnetic field as it cools. The field has reversed fifteen times since. The sentinel reads the stripes on both sides of the axis as a clock.
- **+2.3 Myr.** A caldera eruption in the Ethiopian rift buried the lake basins in ash. The lakes came back within ten thousand years.
- **+3.2 Myr. The twist.** Oceanic crust began forming under Lake Turkana, 600 km from the nearest sea. Magma found the thinnest crust first. An ocean floor with a lake on it.
- **+5 Myr.** The count ran out. Condition three.

### The rift at the anchor

- **Afar.** A young sea 35 km wider than the old depression, with coral and mangrove from the Red Sea. The Bab-el-Mandeb is about four times its old width.
- **Danakil.** The block has torn free and turned. It is an island 500 km long between the gulf and the Red Sea.
- **The Ethiopian rift.** 28 km wider. Still continental. Still 1,100 m up. Its lakes have merged into one, 400 km long. It is opening at 5 mm/yr. Neither ocean nor failed.
- **Turkana.** A deep alkaline lake over new ocean floor. Volcanic islands along its axis.
- **The western branch.** Tanganyika is deeper and 5 Myr older.
- **Victoria.** The lake dried and refilled forty-seven times. Each refilling bred hundreds of new cichlid species within a few thousand years. Each drying ended them.

### Life

- Baboons on Danakil Island, cut off for 3.9 Myr, are smaller and forage the tide line.
- Wild asses work the mainland salt flats.
- Flamingos on the soda lakes, as before.

### The sentinel

Erta Ale's flows buried it twice. It dug out both times. It did not travel. It stood on the Danakil block and the block carried it 30 km from Africa.
